Planning Your Visit

We're excited to welcome you to Messiah Church. We know visiting a church for the first time can feel like a big step, so we want to make it as easy and welcoming as possible for you and your family.

Key Info

  • Stop by Connect Central before or after the service for directions, questions, and next steps.
  • Messiah Kids and Nursery are available so children have a welcoming place prepared for them.
  • Come as you are — you'll see a range of dress, from casual to more traditional.
  • Complete the form below so the team can welcome you personally and make check-in smoother.
  • Add children's names and birth dates if you're bringing kids to save time when you arrive.

What should I expect when I arrive?

When you arrive, stop by Connect Central in the lobby before or after the service. Our team will be glad to answer questions, help you find your way, and share next steps like classes, small groups, and serving opportunities.

Is children's ministry available?

Yes! Messiah Kids gives children a fun, welcoming place to learn about God and discover how much He loves them. Nursery is also available for young children; please visit the Connect Desk for details when you arrive.

What should I wear?

You'll see everything from suits to T-shirts and shorts, so wear what makes you feel comfortable. We care more about welcoming you than what you wear.
